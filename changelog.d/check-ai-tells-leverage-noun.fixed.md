- **`check-ai-tells` no longer flags "leverage" used as a noun**,
  as in "the leverage of an observation" in a statistics page.
  A noun context (a determiner or adjective before it, a link or clause end
  around it, or "leverage value/point/score" after it) is exempt;
  every other use stays flagged, so the verb tell ("leverage the data") is kept
  ([gha#1349](https://github.com/Morrison-Lab/gha/issues/1349)).
