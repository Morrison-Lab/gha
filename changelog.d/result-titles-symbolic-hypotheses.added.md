- `check-result-titles`:
  a third rule flags a result statement that gives a hypothesis only as an inline inequality
  after a cue word, such as `with $X \ge 0$` or `for every $a > 0$`,
  since the statement names each hypothesis in words (Ezra, 2026-11-23;
  psw, "State the title's sentence again in the statement, in words and in symbols").
  Index ranges in subscripts, defining math, and math after "there is" are not flagged.
  It has the statement rule's scope,
  and `<!-- check-result-titles: allow-hypothesis: <reason> -->` in the div exempts it.
