The `epi204-ci.sif` recipe now installs the LaTeX packages that epi204 renders need with `tlmgr` at build time, because TinyTeX is read-only in a job and Quarto cannot install them at render time (gha#1660). The list comes from rendering every epi204 document in both the solution and assign profiles: `koma-script`, `nameref`, `xpatch`, `colortbl`, `makecell`, `multirow`, `pdflscape`, `tabu`, `threeparttable`, `threeparttablex`, `ulem`, `varwidth` and `wrapfig`, plus `accents`, `statmath` and `titlesec` from a scan of epi204's `\usepackage` lines.
