- **`bump-dev-version` and `check-dev-version` run `Rscript --vanilla`, and `bump-dev-version` filters its stdout** (#404). `--vanilla` prevents `.Rprofile` output (such as `renv` bootstrapping banners) from interfering with either composite; `bump-dev-version`'s filter is additional defense-in-depth against such output landing in `$GITHUB_OUTPUT` and failing the step.
